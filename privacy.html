Privacy Policy

StratoshiVibes Bull Run · Last updated: 28 October 2026 (daily track and music added)

Summary

StratoshiVibes Bull Run is a free arcade racing game. It does not ask for an account and does not collect or sell personal data. Connecting a wallet is optional.

What is stored, and where

To keep your progress, the game saves a few small values on your own device using the browser's local storage:

  • your stars and best time on each track, your best time on today's daily track, and your best endless-run distance
  • the tokens you collected in the game, and the car paints you unlocked and chose
  • your language, sound, music, steering and throttle settings
  • if you choose to connect a wallet: your public wallet address and how many StratoshiVibes Vol.1 Degens it holds
  • if you buy an extra: what you bought, the transaction link, and your preferred currency

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it. Tokens in the game are points, not cryptocurrency, and cannot be withdrawn.

Tilt steering

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your phone's motion sensor while you race, to know how far you tilt the phone. These readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Optional wallet connection

You can play everything without a wallet. If you choose to connect one, your wallet shares only your public address with the game.

To check whether you hold StratoshiVibes Vol.1 Degens (which unlocks a holder-only car paint), the game sends your public address to Helius, a Solana data provider, which looks up public blockchain data. Your public address is already visible to anyone on the Solana blockchain. See Helius's privacy policy. Tap Disconnect at any time to remove the address from your device.

Ranking

The ranking is optional. To join, you sign one plain message with your wallet. It is not a transaction and cannot move funds; it only proves the address is yours. After that, the game sends your result at the end of each race (your time on that track, or your endless-run distance) to the StratoshiVibes ranking server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server stores your public wallet address, your best result per board (for the daily track: per day; for the other boards: for the current week and overall), and when you set it. The ranking shows a shortened form of your address to everyone. Tap "Leave the ranking" to stop sending results; to have your results removed, contact us.

Purchases

When you buy an extra, the payment is a public Solana transaction from your wallet to the StratoshiVibes wallet, with a note naming the item (for example "svbullrun:garage"). Like every blockchain transaction, it is permanently visible to anyone. To show prices, the game asks Jupiter for current SOL and SKR prices; this request does not include your address. "Restore purchases" reads your wallet's public transaction history through Helius.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up or email collection (joining the ranking uses your wallet, not an account)
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • No signing requests, except the one ranking sign-in message and the single transfer you approve when you buy an extra in the Shop
  • No tokens or real-money rewards. The only purchases are the optional Shop extras described above, and they change only how your car looks.

Hosting

The game files are served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data in a new way, this page will be updated before that version is released.
